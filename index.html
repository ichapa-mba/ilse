About

Making growth boring, in the best way.

I'm a zero-to-one systems builder. Twice, I've walked into an environment with no growth function, no playbook, and no tooling — once founding a customer base and community from nothing, once as the sole growth owner for a multi-market portfolio of owner-led businesses — and turned ambiguity into a repeatable system before anyone had to ask.

I move across channels rather than specializing in one: funnel testing, audience and community building, partnerships, and hands-on execution alongside strategy. I build the CRM, the funnel, and the KPI dashboard myself, then use the data to make real calls on resourcing, pricing, and expansion — no hand-offs, no analytics team required.

I hold an MBA in Strategic Management & Organizational Leadership, I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, and I'm just as comfortable being the face of a brand — teaching bilingual group fitness classes, building a venue's community in person — as I am behind the dashboard. I'm looking for a small, founder-led team that wants a builder, not a specialist.
